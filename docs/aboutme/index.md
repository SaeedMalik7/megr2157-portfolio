#About Me

I am Saeed Malik, a student at University of North Carolina Charlotte studying Mechanical Engineering with a focus in Motorsports. Ever since I have been a child, I have been into automobiles. My passion for cars has only grown in tandem with my age and is the reason I picked this major. I am studying Mechanical Engineering with the hopes of working in the Automobile Industry one day. I would love to work on the Aerodynamics of an Automobile whether it is for an Original-Equipment-Manufacture or a professional race team. As I progress through the engineering program, I am accumulating certifications in different CAD design software's. I already have a CSWA for Solid works and I am actively working to get certified in Creo. 

As an Engineer, I believe that the best solution to any given problem is a mixture of all the best elements from all the possible solutions put together. During the generating concepts stage of the design process, I believe that one can truly have a great solution if you can somehow take all of your ideas and put together the best individual parts of each of them. If you are able to do that, then you should end up with a truly great product that has minimal flaws. I want to be an Engineer who is known for his innovation along with his ability to problem solve regardless of the situation. I believe that I am a creative individual and given enough time of around a day or two, I can come up with at least two solutions to any given situation. After coming up with different ideas, I think it is important to take something from all the ideas and try to put together a final mega solution. I want to use my creativity and problem solving skills to create innovative aerodynamic parts for automobiles one day.
