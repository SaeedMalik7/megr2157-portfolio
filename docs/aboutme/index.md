**Defending an Engineering Decision**

To Defend an Engineering Decision, means to stand by that said Engineering Decision. I think one can only stand by an Engineering Decision which is positive, I think if you stand by an Engineering Decision that is negative, then you need to reconsider your choices. To stand by an Engineering Decision, means to believe in it when others do not, it means to introduce the ideas to others especially when it may be a foreign concept for them and lastly it means that you are aware that your Engineering Decision solves the problem at hand. I do by it following the three key ideas previously mentioned, and I have done it before, especially in group Engineering projects. In a project everyone can have a good working idea but you should stand by the one you believe in and I think I have done that before, whether it be my own ideas or even others. My judgment may be wrong currently as I do not posses all the required knowledge to make the correct decision to stand by an Engineering Decision or not but I will always try to the best of my current ability.
